Remember 3 things

1
Embedded beats standalone
Microsoft Copilot is #1 in every role (33–42%). AI living inside your workflow wins over AI as a destination.
2
The universal stack is 3 tools
MS Copilot · Perplexity · GitHub Copilot. After that — specialize hard for your target role.
3
It's not too late
Tools launched since 2023 already match pre-GenAI incumbents in adoption. The field is still young.
