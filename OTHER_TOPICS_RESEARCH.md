# Other topics: viral and profitable, with this production tool

Date: 17 Nov 2026. **Limits:** web searches only. YouTube, Social Blade, vidIQ and Google Trends were not reachable from this environment. Most sources are vendor and SEO blogs, so numbers are rough. **No source I found proves that any niche is both easy to enter and highly profitable.** The honest picture is a trade-off, and the table below shows where each topic sits.

## What the evidence says (applies to every topic)
- **Long videos pay, Shorts mostly discover.** AIR's data (300 channels): Education & Science has the highest long-form RPM, median about $10.22; Entertainment about $2.43; Business & Finance about $2.01 in that study. Shorts RPM is roughly 3 to 14% of long-form (range $0.02 to $1.48). [AIR](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/which-youtube-niche-makes-the-most-money-in-2026-ranked-by-real-rpm-and-cpm), [AIR Shorts](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/what-is-youtube-shorts-rpm-in-your-niche-in-2026). Other blogs claim finance pays $15 to $45; the studies disagree, so measure your own.
- **Virality and pay point in opposite directions.** Entertainment, pets, challenges and satisfying clips get the most Shorts views, and educational explainers monetize better. [TubeBuddy](https://www.tubebuddy.com/blog/10-youtube-shorts-niches/), [Teleprompter](https://www.teleprompter.com/blog/best-niches-for-youtube-shorts)
- **Animated explainers are a proven format**, with big incumbents: Psych2Go about 13M subscribers (long-form is most of its views), Economics Explained about 2.9M, How Money Works about 1.8M, Practical Wisdom 1M+. [vidIQ/Social Blade/OutlierKit via search](https://outlierkit.com/channel/psych2go), [MAS Economics](https://maseconomics.com/best-economics-youtube-channels/). That proves demand and shows the competition level.
- **Fastest-growing niches by subscriber growth** (144,000 channels): kids, ASMR, creator education, challenge/stunt and AI. Kids content grows fastest and pays least. [TubeAI](https://tubeai.app/blog/youtube-niches/fastest-growing-youtube-niches), [AIR](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/whos-growing-fastest-on-youtube-and-which-niches-are-hot-in-2025)
- **Policy:** YouTube reviews "inauthentic content" at channel level. Repeated templates and mass production are the risk. A source claims faceless channels are harder to grow in 2026 and 21% of videos shown to new users are AI "slop" (unverified). [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/07/13/youtube-inauthentic-content-monetization-policy-update/)
- **The tool is best at** calm 2D explainers, numbered lessons and history-style cartoons. It is weak at realistic scenes, screen recordings and fast news.

## Recommendation

### 1. Everyday science, narrowed
- **Why:** Education & Science has the highest long-form RPM in AIR's study, curiosity hooks ("Why does your phone get hot when you charge it?") spread well in Shorts, facts are easy to source, and the policy and liability risks are low. It uses the cartoon style well.
- **Competition:** broad science and "what if" is crowded (Kurzgesagt and others). The way in is a **narrow series** with a recognizable look, for example "the hidden science of things at home" (kitchen, bathroom, phone, weather, sleep habits). I could not verify that sub-niche on YouTube.
- **Monetization:** AdSense on long videos (6 to 10 min), affiliate for books and tools, sponsors, and later a newsletter or course.
- **Risk:** health claims. Keep to well-established facts and cite sources.

### 2. How money works (beginner mechanics)
- **Why:** huge evergreen demand, and the cartoon style fits compound interest, inflation, credit scores and how a bank works. Practical Wisdom shows animated money works at 1M+.
- **Competition:** crowded at the top. A narrow angle is needed, for example "money mechanics explained with one object" or "money for first jobs".
- **Risk:** not financial advice; YMYL. No promises of income.

### 3. History micro-stories
- **Why:** strongest viral potential among the educational options (curiosity plus story), and the "1920s Newsreel", Impasto and similar styles fit.
- **Pay:** medium; build toward 8 to 10 minute episodes.
- **Risk:** factual accuracy and the repeated-template policy risk. Vary structure.

## How to decide in one week (I could not do this)
1. For each of A, B and C, search 10 phrases on YouTube. Record the top 10 results: channel size, upload date, views.
2. In vidIQ or Social Blade, find channels under 50,000 subscribers with a video above 100,000 views. Several of these in one sub-niche is the best sign of an open lane.
3. Google Trends, 5 years: check that interest is stable or rising.
4. Make **3 Shorts and 1 long video per topic** with the current tool, publish on 3 separate test channels or playlists, and compare in 30 days: views per video, average view duration, subscribers per 1,000 views.

## Honest bottom line
- There is **no topic with proof of "viral, profitable and uncrowded"**. The choice is between three trade-offs. A and B pay best; C spreads best.
- A single viral spike is luck. A repeatable series with a clear look, a promise and a steady cadence is the thing you can control.
